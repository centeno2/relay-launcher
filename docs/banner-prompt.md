# Banner de Zombies

Archivo: `bo2launcher/assets/zombies-tranzit-banner.png`.

Creado con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`. Ilustración original de comunidad
inspirada en TranZit de BO2 Zombies; no es una captura del juego ni arte oficial.
El archivo se incluye para que el banner funcione sin conexión. Los textos se
dibujan en Qt, con foco, contraste y escalado propios de la aplicación.

## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a finished panoramic desktop game-launcher banner for Relay, a community launcher for Call of Duty Black Ops II Zombies. Create ORIGINAL cinematic fan art inspired by the TranZit mode, not a screenshot or a UI mockup. Wide landscape panorama, approximately 2.5:1. A battered 1960s American transit bus occupies the right half, seen in a low three-quarter front view, its round headlights piercing heavy ashen fog. Several undead silhouettes with subtle glowing amber eyes approach across a cracked abandoned road; one strong zombie silhouette in the far right foreground, no gore or exposed organs. An abandoned roadside diner, broken telegraph poles and a faint orange blaze in the distant mist. Rich, highly detailed premium game key art, tactile rusty metal, volumetric smoke, dramatic but readable shadows, restrained film grain. Palette: charcoal, desaturated green-teal shadows and warm burning amber light. The LEFT THIRD should be low-detail dark smoke and distant atmosphere, deliberately clear for title text to be overlaid in code; place the bus and recognisable action in the right two thirds. Keep focal objects in the central horizontal band so the banner crops elegantly. Atmospheric horror, striking depth, polished art direction. No lettering, no titles, no logos, no watermarks, no interface elements, no borders.
